TkGate browser session

This desktop is private to your session. Files disappear when the session ends.
Save circuits in Documents. Use Kasm's side panel to upload and download files;
download your work before leaving. A browser disconnect is not a backup.

TkGate can be reopened from the desktop icon. Verga and Gmac are available in
the terminal. Example circuits and local documentation are under
/opt/tkgate/share/tkgate. Export printing to a PostScript file; ps2pdf is
available to convert that file to a downloadable PDF. Your computer's printer
is not directly attached to this server.

Modified TkGate source and its GPL license are available at
/usr/share/doc/tkgate/source.tar.gz and /usr/share/doc/tkgate/COPYING.
Copy them to your download location to retrieve them through the side panel.
